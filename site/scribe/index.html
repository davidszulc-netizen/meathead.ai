Scribe

Turns spoken audio from your Ring camera events into readable, searchable text — with optional translation between languages.

Know what was said

Doorbell and camera events become text you can read at a glance and search later — no scrubbing through clips.

Understand every language spoken at your door

Optional translation renders conversations in the language you read, even when they happened in one you don't speak.

Privacy first

Audio is processed and then deleted. Only text is kept, for a retention period you control, and you can delete everything with one action. We never train AI models on your data and never sell it.

Scribe is currently in private development and testing for the Ring Appstore. It is not yet available to the public.
