Scribe connects to your Ring account only after you authorize it through Ring's account-linking process, and uses Ring's official developer APIs.

Transcripts and translations are produced by automated AI systems and may contain errors, omissions, or mistranslations. They are provided for convenience only. They are not a verbatim record, and they must not be relied upon for legal, evidentiary, security-response, safety-critical, or emergency purposes. Always review the original recording before acting on anything important.

Factors including background noise, music, distance from the microphone, overlapping speakers, accents, and audio quality materially affect accuracy. Some recordings cannot be transcribed usefully at all.
